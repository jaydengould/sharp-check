# P&L rules (agreed 2026-09-30)

- **Stake** = entry cost + entry fees.
- **Realized P&L** = exit proceeds + settlement payout − stake − exit fees.
- **Partial exits** use average cost.
- **Open bets** are excluded from realized P&L. They are shown separately as unrealized, marked at mid.
- **ROI** = realized P&L ÷ total stake (turnover). It is not computed against bankroll.
- **Two headline P&L numbers** (decided 2026-10-02):
  - **Betting P&L**: every bet scored normally, including bets made with bonus money. Used for ROI, CLV and expected-vs-actual wins.
  - **Own-money P&L** = (account value − locked bonus) − (deposits − withdrawals). Locked bonus and winnings that are still locked aren't the user's money, so losing them costs nothing; they count once released. Computed per venue from balance snapshots, so there's no per-bet bonus tracing (the data can't support it: trades don't record their funding).
  - Bonus contribution = own-money P&L − betting P&L.
  - Kalshi perps transfers count like deposits and withdrawals in own-money P&L. Perps trading is out of scope.
- **Bankroll growth** is a separate metric from `cash_ledger`. Bankroll = account cash + open position cost, both venues combined.
- **Voids/postponements** pay whatever the venue pays. They are excluded from CLV and win rate.
- **Market CLV** (phase 4): close = the same venue's mid at `start_time` on your side (YES mid, or 1 − YES mid for NO). CLV = close − average entry price, fees excluded. Headline = CLV $ (Σ entry qty × CLV) next to realized P&L, both over closed bets only, plus mean CLV in cents with a 95% CI and the share beating the close.
  - **After fees** (decided 2026-10-03, the +EV check): close mid − stake ÷ entry qty, i.e. net of entry fees. Shown first.
  - **Price move**: your side's close ask − entry price. Timing only: a fair taker bet with no move scores 0, so it flatters by about half a spread. Only pregame singles on games with type moneyline/spread/total/prop; closed-early bets keep their CLV.
- **Live vs pregame:** a bet is live if its first fill is at or after the game's `start_time`. Live bets and bets with no start time get no CLV but count in everything else.
- **Win rate** is always shown against expected wins (the sum of entry prices), with a confidence interval, never on its own. A bet counts when its outcome is 0/1: held to settlement (a win = payout > 0), or cashed out early and judged by how its market finished (would it have won if held; `markets.yes_value`). Voids and cash-outs whose market hasn't resolved (or never resolves) don't count. Settled-only was biased low because cash-outs are mostly winning positions (2026-10-04: 18 vs 29.1 expected settled-only, below the CI; 30 vs 36.8 judging cash-outs too).
- **Segments** show n and a CI, and are hidden when n < 30.
- **CIs on CLV and line-shop gaps** (decided 2026-10-08) group bets by game: singles by `game_id`, parlays by shared leg games, chained (`metrics.parlay_groups`). Half-width = the wider of the plain and cluster-robust ones (with few groups the clustered one is noisy and can come out narrower). On 2026-10-08 it moved singles CLV from ±0.67 to ±0.69 and nothing else noticeably. The win-count CI still assumes independent bets; same-game bets make it a bit narrow, and fixing that needs joint win probabilities. Groups are per venue until phase 5 (see roadmap latent issues).
- **Habit metrics** (phase 6, `sharp_check/habits.py`):
  - Bankroll curve = ledger cash + realized P&L of closed bets, both venues. Placing a bet doesn't move it. Per venue (Overview switch): that venue's ledger and bets only. One point per timestamp (a deposit and its fee net out). Its last point must equal reported cash + open cost net of partial exits; that confirms only the end point. Between a partial exit and the close, the curve counts the exited part at cost until the bet closes.
  - "After a win/loss" = sign of the realized P&L of the latest bet (either venue) closed strictly before this bet opened. 0 or a void = after a push. Stake % mostly reflects funding habits: deposits made right before a bet push it toward 100% of bankroll.
  - Cash-out value (hindsight) = what you got for the part you sold (proceeds − exit fees) minus what holding that part would have paid, judged by the market's final result. Positive = cashing out helped. Covers full and partial exits; void or unresolved markets are pending and excluded. Luck-dominated at small n, so always shown with its CI. Cashing out at a fair price is break-even in expectation; its real cost is exit fees plus the exit spread (below).
  - Stake % of bankroll uses the bankroll just before the bet.
  - Timing buckets: < 15 min, 15 min–1 h, 1–6 h, 6–24 h, > 24 h before kickoff (lower bound inclusive), pregame bets only; CLV per bucket uses singles only, under the n ≥ 30 rule.
  - Execution cost: fees by venue × singles/parlays. Fees % of stake: all fill fees (entry and exit) over bet stakes (entry fees included). Estimated maker saving (taker fills on singles only) = fee paid − maker fee on the same fill: Kalshi 0.0175 × C × p(1−p) assumed on every series (0 on `quadratic` series, so understated), Polymarket rebate 0.0125 × C × p(1−p) (schedule effective 2026-10-01). Assumes the limit order fills at the same price.
- **Parlays** are one bet each. They get P&L, ROI and expected-vs-actual wins (from the entry price), and market CLV when eligible (below). Every headline metric is also shown split into parlays vs. singles.
- **Parlay CLV** (decided 2026-10-03): only pregame, non-void parlays whose legs are all game markets from different games (same-game legs are correlated; no correlation model). Each leg's mid on the parlay's side (NO leg = 1 − YES mid). Fair at close = Π leg mids at each leg's own kickoff; fair at entry = Π leg mids when the parlay was placed. Markup = entry − fair at entry (positive = overpaid); markup % = Σ markup ÷ Σ fair at entry. Leg movement = fair at close − fair at entry. CLV = fair at close − entry = movement − markup; after fees uses stake ÷ qty like singles. Leg quotes must be ≤ 5¢ wide and ≤ 15 min old at both times (one wide leg can dominate the product); otherwise no CLV, listed as "no usable leg quote" (or "waiting for kickoff" while a leg hasn't started). Other parlays are listed with a reason.
- **Spread** (phase 8a, decided 2026-10-05): every fill is "acquire `outcome` at `price`", so cost = price − mid of that outcome, per contract, fees excluded, signed (negative = the price moved your way within the minute; never clipped). Exits use the same formula (an exit acquires the other side). Quote key = the fill time floored to the minute (`archive.fill_key`); Polymarket one minute earlier, since a point may mark either end of its minute. Singles: the latest point at or before the key decides; it must be two-sided and ≤ 5 min old, else no cost ("one-sided quote" / "stale quote" / "no quote"). Never fall back to an older quote: a decided Kalshi market quotes ask 1.00 and an older quote is from before the result. Width isn't limited (it's what's measured). Parlay entries: mid = `fair_entry` (so entry spread = the CLV markup). Parlay exits (CLV-eligible parlays only): mid = 1 − Π leg values on the parlay's side, per leg from its latest point in the hour: pinned within 1¢ of 0/1 → the leg's final result if it agrees; no points on a fetched Kalshi page after kickoff (market closed) → its final result; else ≤ 5¢ wide and ≤ 5 min old. Any unpriced fill leaves its bet's entry or exit spread NULL; Habits sums priced fills and shows priced x of y. In play = a fill at or after kickoff (per fill, so a pregame bet's in-game cash-out is in play). In-play singles are left out of the $ totals and the cash-out exit-spread total and shown as median ¢ per contract per venue: the mid is up to 1 minute old (2 on Polymarket), and a score inside that minute swamps the spread (a sale seconds after a goal read +48.5¢; a Kalshi exit −34¢). Nearly all cash-outs are in play, so the median is the usable number. Parlays stay summed in play: decided legs take their final result and open legs must be ≤ 5¢ wide and ≤ 5 min old. A fill reads quotes only if an archived page's window covers its key, else "no quote".
- **Line shop** (phase 8b, decided 2026-10-05): all-in per contract = ask + taker fee. Kalshi fee = 0.07 × series `fee_multiplier` (live: current; past bets: in effect at the first fill, from the archived fee-change history) × C × p(1−p), rounded up to the cent per order, at the stake (live: $10 = `match.USUAL_STAKE`; past bets: your stake) and spread over its contracts. Polymarket fee = θ × p(1−p): live θ = the market's `feeCoefficient` (missing → the latest `match.PM_THETA`); past θ = `match.PM_THETA` read off your fills (0.05 before 2026-07-01, 0.06 to 2026-09-15, 0.0695 after; no fills before 2026-06-22, assumed 0.05). Outcomes pair across venues by key (team role, exact line, normalized player name + threshold); buying NO costs 1 − YES bid. Teams map by away/home role, oriented by team code (`match.orientation`: Kalshi's game code starting with Polymarket's away code or ending with its home code; the reverse = swapped, as for some neutral-site games); when no code matches, the live checker takes the order whose moneyline mids agree and a past bet needs its own mid (else "team order unclear"). A pair whose mids differ by more than 15¢ is dropped ("side check failed"). Live checker: gap under 0.5¢ = no clear edge; thin = under $10 offered at the ask. Past singles (`line_shop`): yours = stake ÷ entry qty; theirs = the other venue's ask for your outcome + modeled fee at your first fill's quote key, under the spread rule's quote rule (latest point decides, two-sided, ≤ 5 min); cheapest priced ref wins (a team win is YES on its market or NO on the other team's); side check against your side's mid before that fill. Gap = yours − theirs, positive = the other venue was cheaper; left on the table = Σ positive gap × your contracts. Pregame summed with a 95% CI; in play as a median only. Entries only (cash-outs are the exit spread). Reasons: game not on other venue, no matching line, prop type not covered, prop not listed, no quote, stale quote, one-sided quote, side check failed, team order unclear, not fetched yet. The live checker drops a cached game once it starts; Refresh re-prices the chosen game and reloads the game list only when none is chosen.
